About Me

Hi! I'm Luis Antonio Arreola Gutiérrez, a Senior Full-Stack Developer specialized in Ruby on Rails, with over 10 years of experience building SaaS platforms from backend to frontend and more than 5 years working remotely.

Although my main stack is Rails, I adapt quickly to different languages and platforms: at Reservamos I was hired as a Rails developer and worked on Elixir/Phoenix, Python/Django and Rails teams, and also migrated a site to WordPress. My performance reviews highlighted my ability to handle several projects at once, even across different stacks.

I especially enjoy parallelism, background jobs and process automation. Lately I've focused on building skills for AI agents (Claude Code, Cursor, Codex) and AI-assisted development with OpenSpec documentation.

I strongly believe in collaborative teamwork, leveraging each member's strengths to achieve the best results.
